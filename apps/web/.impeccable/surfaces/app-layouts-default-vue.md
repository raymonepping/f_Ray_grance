# F-Ray-grance console surface brief

Scope: the whole authenticated console (all routes under app/pages) plus sign-in. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: the single owner-collector at their Mac, often in the evening. Job: verify an exact fragrance, read how it is built, record their relationship to it, compare two, keep seasons, learn. Constraints: PRODUCT.md truth (provenance labels, unknown stays unknown, no fake imagery, append-only history), WCAG 2.2 AA both themes, strict CSP, no external fonts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every fragrance is read like a sample on the instrument: its pyramid becomes a chromatogram of labelled peaks, its provenance the calibration line. Refuses the category default of bottle-card grids with star ratings and a marketing hero.

OWN-WORLD: Anodised graphite bays (#0b0e12 ground, #12161c panels) with etched hairline scales, ice-white ink, steel-grey secondary ink, and a phosphor-lime signal reserved for live, verified state only. Geist for the interface, Geist Mono for every measurement. Fixed column grids where only values change; rank by weight, case and reversal before size. Light theme is calibrated lab paper with the same grammar.

STORY: The owner sees their collection as a sample table, opens a fragrance and watches it scan into peaks, trusts each reading because its source is etched beside it, then records what they own and when they want it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Collection at 1440x900: left channel rail with the Spectral F mark; top a thin instrument header with search and one primary action; the sample table fills the bay, one fixed-column row per bottle with a micro-chromatogram of its tiers, name, house, concentration, state and rating; readout counters sit above the table at the right.

FORM: Scent Spectrometer, grounded list position 4 of 7, seed key 11469ade. Signature interaction: The Scan, a single scan line sweeps the chromatogram as data arrives and peaks rise once; reduced motion renders instantly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
