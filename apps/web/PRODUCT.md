# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One owner-collector, the person who runs F-Ray-grance on their own Mac. They use it at a desk on the Mac today; access from their iPhone comes later. Their job is to understand their own fragrance rotation, not to shop or to perform for others.

## Product Purpose

A self-hosted, private fragrance-intelligence console. It lets the owner verify exact fragrance identities, record owned, sampled, wishlist, rejected and previously owned bottles with an unbroken history, compare two fragrances side by side, keep explicit seasonal preferences, and learn the craft in the Scent Academy. Success means the owner trusts every fact on screen and can answer "what does this smell like next to what I own, and when do I want it?" in seconds.

## Positioning

Exactness and provenance. Every fact is labelled by origin (Scento, official, derived, personal), unknown stays unknown, near-name flankers are never substituted, and personal history is append-only. It runs entirely on the owner's own machine.

## Operating Context

Single owner signed in on a loopback-only local console. Data comes from the owner's own entries plus on-demand lookups from the Scento Perfume API (attributed, English). Optional local embeddings power discovery. Backups are manual. Sessions are short and task-shaped: add a bottle, check a pyramid, compare two scents, record a blotter test.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Collection with states owned, previously owned, sampled, wishlist and rejected, and append-only history; ratings are separate from ownership.
- Discover: exact-identity resolution with explicit review states for near matches; manual drafts with no invented notes.
- Fragrance detail with a sourced note pyramid, traits on a 0 to 5 indicative scale (unknown is "-", never zero) and per-field provenance.
- Compare: exactly two fragrances in v0.1.
- My DNA: four explicit seasonal profiles, never inferred from ownership.
- Scent Academy: six original lessons, glossary, reference lookups and private "Train My Nose" practice.
- Perfume images (Phase 12): one primary image per fragrance or variant, source-attributed.
- Never a shop, a social feed, a price tracker or a daily wear journal. No public sign-up.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: F-Ray-grance. Tagline: "Don't just track your fragrances. Understand your rotation."
- Logo: a "Spectral F", a geometric F whose bars read as spectral lines, a ray split through a prism (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-25).
- The owner asked for a console that feels classy, modern, futuristic and sleek, and for a full replacement of the previous visual world.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real verified catalogue data: Rabanne Phantom in Red (parfum) with a sourced pyramid; Scento fixtures for lavender, sillage, green leaf and vetiver.
- No licensed bottle photography yet; images arrive through Phase 12's owner upload and approved sources. Nothing may stand in for a real bottle photo.

## Product Principles

1. Truth before polish: provenance, unknowns and history are always visible and never faked.
2. The fragrance is the subject; the interface is the instrument.
3. Fast, task-shaped sessions on a personal machine.
4. Private by default: personal notes never leave the Mac.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes, keyboard-complete, reduced motion and reduced transparency respected, 200% zoom, 320 px reflow without page-wide horizontal scroll.
